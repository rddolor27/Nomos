# Round 8 · Cultures: questions and status

The owner wants blobs to have their own customs. They chose learned cultures: people belong to fictional cultures with harmless customs such as favourite foods, festivals, music, naming and home region. Children learn them, and people can mix or switch. Customs shape preferences only, never ability, honesty, work or crime. Culture is independent of the six cosmetic body hues and is never shown by clothing or body. No culture maps to a real-world ethnicity, nation or religion.

This is a lean round: three researchers, then a report, a fact-check and plan tasks tagged (R8), following `/research-round`.

| # | Question | Notes | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How do cultures pass between generations, spread, mix and change with migration, and how should Nomos model it deterministically? | `notes/transmission.md` | Done, with a prototype in `prototypes/transmission/` |
| 2 | How should customs feed preferences (food demand, festivals, music and services, names, home regions) without touching ability or crime, and how is that checked? | `notes/customs-preferences.md` | Done, with prototypes in `prototypes/customs/` |
| 3 | What do games and explorables teach about fictional cultures, and how does Nomos keep them respectful and free of real-world mapping? | `notes/prior-art-ethics.md` | Done |
