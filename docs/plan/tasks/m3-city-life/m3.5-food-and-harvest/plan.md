# M3.5 Food and harvest: implementation brief

## Approach

- **The grain season (R6, Calendar):**
  - crops accrue daily on M3.1's farms;
  - the harvest lands once a year, over 9–14 days of autumn, as dated grain lots;
  - the yield scales by Q16 soil fertility and a keyed weather draw with SD 0.13–0.22, part regional and part local;
  - soil fertility has a floor at 0.35–0.45 of manured yield, never zero.
- **Production shows through places only.**
  - Stall stock pips show production, and fields, forests and docks empty and regrow.
  - At most four or five removable job items are drawn, none black.
  - The art exists; wire it in.
- **Pantries:**
  - each pantry holds at most 8 `Uint32` lots, packed `exp:16 | cat:3 | grade:2 | storage:2 | qty:9` as in M2.4, sorted by expiry;
  - lots merge only on equal keys;
  - a full pantry merges into the same-category lot with the earlier expiry.
- **Shelf lives:**
  - the 6 × 3 shelf-life table from FoodKeeper and the FDA chart drives freshness;
  - storage moves convert the remaining life by integer proportion;
  - only staples in poor storage lose 0.015–0.04% a day to pests, by keyed stochastic rounding;
  - perishables have no daily random loss: they spoil by hard expiry at the day boundary.
- **Household food quality:**
  - a 6-bit weekly category mask: "varied" is at least 4 of 6, "monotonous" at most 2;
  - an 8-item monthly FIES-style tally per household;
  - food poisoning at 0.01% per meal, ×10 when stale, and 2% for spoiled food eaten when starving.
- **Day slices:** pantry expiry follows M0.3's owner ruling on how late spoilage may land in sliced day work.

## Interfaces and data

- **Crop state per field:** `growth` (Int32, Q16), `fertilityQ16` and `harvestDay`.
- **Household columns:** `pantry` (8 × `Uint32`), `varietyMask` (`Uint8`, 6 bits used) and `fiesTally` (`Uint8`, the 8 monthly flags).
- **Food ledger:** M2.4's daily portion identity, extended with harvest as "produced" and household spoilage as "spoiled".

## Tests for the exit checks

- `household spoilage with cool storage`: over 50 paired seeds in the city preset, households spoil 3–6% of purchased portions.
- `no-fridge weekly-market variant`: with ambient storage and a weekly market, at least 15% of perishables spoil.
- `no daily random loss for perishables`: a perishable lot's quantity changes only by sale, consumption or hard expiry. A property test over 10,000 lot histories finds no other change.
- `food insecurity in range`: in the default town over 50 paired seeds, 5–15% of households score 4 or more on the tally.
- `harvest once a year, stores carry winter`:
  - over 20 seeds × 3 years, grain is harvested only on autumn days and on 9–14 of them;
  - no household runs out of grain stores before the next spring's planting in the default preset.
- `pantry merges`: unit tests for equal-key merge, full-pantry merge into the earlier same-category lot, and expiry order.

## Risks and unknowns

- **M0.3's ruling on late spoilage** must exist before pantry expiry is final (round 6 conflict e).
- **Round 6 rates set for a 365-day year** must be re-read as per day or per year first (M2.7's verify-first item), especially the ≤ 7% pest loss a season.
- **Weather SD 0.13–0.22 is a band, not a value.** Pick one per biome in the step plan and log it as a knob.

## Open questions

- **Owner:** What does a food-poisoning event do before any health system exists? Round 6 makes it a health event, but M3 has no health model ([R6 food notes](../../../../research/round-6-goods-and-wellbeing/notes/food-quality-spoilage.md), part c). Suggested: one sick day at home, logged as an event, and nothing more until health arrives. Needed before: building.
- **Measure:** Where does a year's grain wait between harvest and sale? One 9–14-day harvest must feed the town all year, but a shop shelf holds at most 32 × 511 = 16,352 portions (computed). Suggested: a store per farm or granary holding one portion count per harvest day, since grain's 365-day use-by outlasts three game years. Needed before: the step plan.

## Implementation notes

Suggestions for the step plan, which makes the final call.

- **Build order:** pantries and their merge rules first, on M2.4's lot word. Then the shelf-life table, household spoilage with its two checks, the harvest and weather, quality and the tally, and the place visuals last.
- **Reuse:** M2.4's lot word, FEFO, spoilage sweep and portion ledger; M0.2's keyed stochastic rounding and tables; M3.1's farms; the existing field and pip art.
- **Keep it simple:** one spoilage sweep for shelves and pantries, never a second path.
- **Pitfalls:**
  - Test the town's grain stock through winter, not each pantry, since the 5–15% insecurity target means some households run short by design.
  - The tally's monthly window follows M2.1's month constant.
  - Key regional weather on (seed, region, year) and local weather on (seed, farm, year), never on farm order.
- **Hard and easy parts:** hitting the 3–6% spoilage and 5–15% insecurity bands together is the hard part. The mask, the tally and the poisoning odds are mechanical.
