# M7.7 Spin-up and patrols

Part of [M7 Country of ledgers](../milestone.md).

Needs M5's defence budget. It also builds the route ledgers that patrols need and M8 draws, adding about a day.

- **Builds:**
  - the 50–100-year spin-up (5,600–11,200 days) and country skip-ahead in 112-day years (Calendar);
  - the route ledgers that patrols need and M8 draws: traffic, bandit pressure, patrols, and true and recorded incidents on each route (R4, Military);
  - garrison posts on settlement ledgers, and a patrol intensity on each route ledger from nearby garrisons and the defence budget: raids fall as patrols rise, true and recorded raids stay apart, and records follow reports and sightings (Military).
- **Borders, settled by the owner on 9 October 2026:** a world holds 3–5 countries, so a border is the land boundary between two of them. Garrison towns are each country's capital and its coastal or border towns, and a border town lies within 3 cells of a border, a proposed reach that M8.8 shares. Neighbours are never at war, and a road that crosses a border is patrolled from garrisons on both sides (Military, Countries).
- **Exit checks:**
  - on paired seeds, more patrols cut true raids, and recorded raids rise or fall with sightings (Military).
