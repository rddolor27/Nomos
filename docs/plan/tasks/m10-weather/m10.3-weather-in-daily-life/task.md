# M10.3 Weather in daily life

Part of [M10 Weather](../milestone.md).

Builds on M10.1's daily weather. It is built only if the owner chooses routines; with looks only, it closes with nothing built and its exit check dropped.

- **Builds:**
  - if the owner chooses routines, weather changes where people go: shelter in rain and storms, thinner markets and festivals, and slower travel; weather never changes crime directly, only who is out and who sees it (Weather).
- **Needs:** M10.1's weather; M1.2's bet-card shell; M3.2's routines and utility scoring; M3.7's festivals; M4.2's crime, witnesses and reporting; M5.5's exposure lens.
- **Owner decision first:** whether weather changes what people do, or only how the world looks and sounds.
- **Verify first:** research round 10's evidence on weather, time outdoors and crime, which sets how strongly rain keeps people in.
- **Exit checks:**
  - if weather changes routines: on paired seeds, rain lowers outdoor hours, and true against recorded crime is reported by weather, never by culture (Weather).
