## Decisions

These are the agents' rulings. The owner can overturn any of them.
- **The town is generated, not LDtk.** The two LDtk-worded M0 items are ticked, because the generated town meets their intent (round 9).
- **The frame budget runs as its own Playwright project,** after the engine projects. Alongside them its p95 reached 15 ms; alone it held 0.5 ms.
- **The harness listens on IPv4,** because Firefox sometimes failed to reach a `::1`-only server.
- **The ground is an unhashed input,** so a restore must pass the same ground. A map hash joins M6's deferred save check.
- **lil-gui's LOD control is dropped,** since the skin toggle's Auto owns LOD. Node's floor rises to 24.5, for size-limit 14.2.
